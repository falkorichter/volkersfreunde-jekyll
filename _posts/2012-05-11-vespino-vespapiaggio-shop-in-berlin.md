Vespino – a nice shop that repairs your Piaggio/Vespa scooter. If you want to reach the guy you would need to call him directly, 0179 4562330 is the cell number. He used to be in [Kastanienallee](http://www.qype.com/place/128493-Vespino-Berlin) but moved to Ostkreuz ([Hauptstrasse 1g-h, Berlin](http://maps.google.de/maps/place?q=Hauptstrasse+1g-h,+berlin&hl=de&ftid=0x47a84efabda4dd95:0x429ada42c52b5fc))
