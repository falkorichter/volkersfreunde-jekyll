If you´re in Berlin and you need some sort of video/photo equipment, I can highly recommend [25p cine support](http://www.25p-berlin.de/). They even have a quadrotor to carry a GoPro that you can rent.

I´ve rented a battery, and even though I had it for 5 days, they only charged me for 3 days of useage.
